New Here? Start with this 60-second tour

No technical skills needed — we'll walk you through it

Create Your Account

Click "Book an Appointment" or "Register". Fill in your name, email, and phone number. It takes 1 minute.

Log in, choose a doctor and time that suits you. Confirm. That's it — no more waiting in line.
